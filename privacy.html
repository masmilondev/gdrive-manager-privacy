gdrive-manager privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What the app accesses

gdrive-manager runs on its owner's own computer. With the Google Drive permission you grant when you sign in, it lists, uploads and removes files in your Drive, and only when you run a command.

Where your data goes

Files move directly between your computer and Google Drive. The app does not send your files, file names or Google account details to the developer or to any other third party.

What is stored

Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your own computer. Nothing is stored on a server run by the developer.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions.
